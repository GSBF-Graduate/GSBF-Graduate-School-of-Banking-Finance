The MBA in Venture Strategy is the first program at Nolan University designed, delivered, and governed jointly by the Graduate School of Banking & Finance and the School of Entrepreneurship & Venture Creation.

🎯 Program Vision

To develop the next generation of venture leaders who can seamlessly navigate both sides of the table—building transformative companies and deploying capital strategically.

This is not a traditional MBA. It is an intensive, immersive experience that builds dual competence in venture finance and venture creation, with a focus on African markets and opportunities.

📖 Complete Course Catalog

Explore our comprehensive curriculum, designed by faculty from both schools.

FIN701: Venture Finance I: Capital Markets & Valuation • 3 Credits GSBF

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: None ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: This course provides a rigorous foundation in financial analysis, valuation methods, and capital structure decisions for ventures. Students learn to build financial models, analyze investment opportunities, and understand how capital markets interact with early-stage companies.

Learning Outcomes:

  • Apply discounted cash flow, comparable company, and precedent transaction valuation methods
  • Analyze capital structure decisions for ventures at different stages
  • Build integrated financial models for early-stage companies
  • Understand how public and private capital markets interact with ventures

Assessment: Valuation project (40%), midterm exam (30%), final exam (30%)

FIN702: Venture Finance II: Deal Structuring & Term Sheets • 3 Credits GSBF

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: FIN701 ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: Deep dive into the mechanics of venture deals. Students analyze term sheets, negotiate deal terms, and understand the implications of different financing structures for both investors and founders.

Learning Outcomes:

  • Analyze and negotiate term sheet provisions
  • Build and maintain cap tables through multiple financing rounds
  • Understand liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, and governance rights
  • Structure deals that align investor and founder incentives

FIN703: Venture Capital & Private Equity • 3 Credits GSBF

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: FIN701 ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: Comprehensive overview of the venture capital and private equity industry, including fund structures, LP-GP dynamics, fundraising, due diligence, and portfolio management.

ENT701: Venture Creation I: Opportunity Recognition • 3 Credits ENT

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: None ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: This course explores the entrepreneurial mindset and the process of identifying and evaluating business opportunities. Students learn to recognize patterns, assess market needs, and validate ideas through customer discovery.

Learning Outcomes:

  • Develop an entrepreneurial mindset and identify personal strengths
  • Apply frameworks for opportunity recognition and evaluation
  • Conduct customer interviews and synthesize findings
  • Articulate a clear value proposition

ENT702: Venture Creation II: Product-Market Fit • 3 Credits ENT

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: ENT701 ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: Achieving product-market fit and designing growth strategies. Covers metrics, channels, and scaling tactics for startups.

VS701: Entrepreneurial Finance • 3 Credits Joint

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: FIN701, ENT701 ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: Financial decision-making for early-stage ventures, taught jointly by finance and entrepreneurship faculty. Students learn to evaluate financial trade-offs from both investor and founder perspectives.

LAW701: Legal & Regulatory Environment • 3 Credits Joint

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: None ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: Legal foundations for ventures, including entity formation, intellectual property, securities law, contracts, and exit strategies. Designed for non-lawyers.

VS702: Impact & ESG Investing • 3 Credits Joint

▼
📚 Core Course 📊 Prerequisite: FIN701 ⏱️ 45 Contact Hours

Course Description: Measuring and managing social and environmental returns alongside financial returns. Covers impact measurement frameworks, ESG integration, and blended finance structures.

VS710: Venture Practicum (Capstone) • 6 Credits Joint

▼
📚 Capstone 📊 Prerequisite: All core courses ⏱️ 90 Contact Hours

Course Description: The culminating experience where students work in teams on a real-world venture or investment project. Projects are sponsored by industry partners. Final presentations to a panel of investors and founders.
