WHO WE ARE

GSBF – Graduate School of Banking & Finance is founded by Lewis Mwaba under M. Lewis Consulting & Research and is operated by a group of investment practitioners and mentors drawn from the global workforce.

We are a finance career academy and professional certification platform positioning ourselves as the Human Capital Infrastructure for Finance Careers in Africa. GSBF is built to bridge the gap between academic finance education and real-world investment practice. Our mission is to equip graduates and professionals with practical, industry-relevant skills required to thrive in modern financial markets.

Through structured programs, mentorship, and hands-on training, GSBF prepares participants for careers in investment banking, private equity, venture capital, wealth management, and finance-driven entrepreneurship.

Our ecosystem combines global expertise, professional mentorship, and practical learning frameworks to create a new standard for finance education and career development.
