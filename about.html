About GSBF

WHO WE ARE

GSBF – Graduate School of Banking & Finance is founded by Mwaba Lewis under M. Lewis Consulting & Research and is operated by a group of investment practitioners and mentors drawn from the global workforce.

We are a learning and career infrastructure platform built to bridge the gap between academic finance education and real-world investment practice. Our mission is to equip graduates and professionals with practical, industry-relevant skills required to thrive in modern financial markets.

Through structured programs, mentorship, and hands-on training, GSBF prepares participants for careers in investment banking, private equity, venture capital, wealth management, and finance-driven entrepreneurship.

Our ecosystem combines global expertise, professional mentorship, and practical learning frameworks to create a new standard for finance education and career development.

GSBF (Graduate School of Banking & Finance) is a brand operated by M. Lewis Consulting & Research.

All programs, services, and materials provided through GSBF are administered by M. Lewis Consulting & Research under applicable laws and regulations.
