Program Overview

GSBF is an independent learning platform focused on preparing serious learners for elite finance careers. We teach real-world frameworks used in investment banking, private equity, venture capital, and wealth management.

Our training combines structured curriculum, applied case work, professional certification, and career positioning strategy.

Certification Pathway

CFFA — Certified Finance Foundations Associate

Entry-level professional foundation covering financial markets, accounting basics, valuation principles, and investment frameworks.

Duration: 8–10 weeks

Investment: $600 – $900

CIP — Certified Investment Professional

Intermediate training focused on deal analysis, valuation modeling, portfolio strategy, and investment decision-making.

Duration: 10–12 weeks

Investment: $1,500 – $2,500

CAIP — Certified Advanced Investment Professional

Advanced professional training in transaction modeling, capital structuring, and high-level investment frameworks.

Duration: 12–16 weeks

Investment: $3,500 – $6,000
