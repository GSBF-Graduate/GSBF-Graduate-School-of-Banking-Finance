The GSBF Professional Program

GSBF is an independent learning platform dedicated to equipping young graduates and professionals with the knowledge, skills, and network to succeed in elite finance careers or build businesses around financial systems.

The program focuses on real-world frameworks used in investment banking, private equity, venture capital, and wealth advisory — not academic theory alone.

Certification Pathway

CFFA — Certified Finance Foundations Associate

Entry-level training in financial analysis, markets, and investment fundamentals. Designed for beginners entering finance.

CIP — Certified Investment Professional

Intermediate professional training in valuation, deal analysis, and portfolio strategy for IB/PE/VC pathways.

CAIP — Certified Advanced Investment Professional

Advanced-level training in transaction modeling, capital strategy, and investment decision frameworks.

Admissions

GSBF operates a selective admissions process designed to identify serious and committed learners.

Applicants may qualify through academic credentials or demonstrated practitioner capability via assessment and interview.

We value merit, discipline, and long-term professional intent.
