CFFA — Certified Finance Foundations Associate

Duration: 6–8 weeks

Investment: $150 – $300
Modules:
  • Module 1: Financial Literacy & Accounting Foundations
    • Financial statements (Income, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
    • Ratio analysis
    • Business performance interpretation
  • Module 2: Excel & Analytical Tools
    • Excel modeling basics
    • Data organization
    • Financial calculations
  • Module 3: Introduction to Valuation
    • Time value of money
    • Comparable company analysis
    • Basic discounted cash flow
  • Module 4: Investment Thinking
    • Risk vs return
    • Market structures
    • Investment frameworks
  • Module 5: Professional Communication
    • Writing financial summaries
    • Presenting analysis
    • Business professionalism
Exam & Assessment:
  • Written Exam: 40%
  • Practical Modeling Assignment: 40%
  • Case Presentation: 20%
  • Passing Threshold: 70%
Career Pathways:
  • Junior analyst internships
  • Finance assistant roles
  • Entry-level investment support
  • Advancement to Tier 2

Marketing: “Your first professional step into serious finance.”

CIP — Certified Investment Professional

Duration: 8–12 weeks

Investment: $400 – $800
Modules:
  • Module 1: Advanced Financial Modeling
    • Integrated financial models
    • Scenario analysis
  • Module 2: Valuation Mastery
    • Advanced DCF
    • Comparable transactions
    • LBO fundamentals
  • Module 3: Deal Analysis
    • Investment memos
    • Risk frameworks
    • Due diligence basics
  • Module 4: Market Strategy
    • Industry analysis
    • Competitive positioning
  • Module 5: Simulated Transactions
    • Deal execution exercises
    • Team collaboration
Exam & Assessment:
  • Capstone Deal Simulation: 50%
  • Investment Memo: 30%
  • Oral Defense: 20%
  • Passing Threshold: 75%
Career Pathways:
  • Investment analyst roles
  • Corporate finance teams
  • Venture startup analysis
  • Advancement to Tier 3

Marketing: “Operate like a real investment professional.”

CAIP — Certified Advanced Investment Practitioner

Duration: 12–16 weeks

Investment: $900 – $1,800
Modules:
  • Module 1: Advanced Transactions
    • Deal structuring
    • Negotiation strategy
  • Module 2: Portfolio & Strategy
    • Asset allocation
    • Portfolio risk
  • Module 3: Leadership in Finance
    • Team decision-making
    • Investment leadership
  • Module 4: Real-world Investment Project
    • Multi-week live simulation
Exam & Assessment:
  • Major Deal Project: 60%
  • Panel Evaluation: 25%
  • Portfolio Submission: 15%
  • Passing Threshold: 80%
Career Pathways:
  • Senior analyst roles
  • Investment management
  • Entrepreneurial finance leadership
  • GSBF Fellowship eligibility

Marketing: “Elite training for high-performance finance leaders.”
