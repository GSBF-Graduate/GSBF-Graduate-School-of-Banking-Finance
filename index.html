What GSBF Is

GSBF is the Graduate School of Banking & Finance, one of four schools at Nolan University, serving as a finance career academy and professional certification platform that positions itself as the Human Capital Infrastructure for Finance Careers in Africa. It is designed to guide and train graduates and professionals, from foundational finance knowledge to advanced investment expertise, providing practical skills, mentorship, structured career pathways, and career support to thrive in Investment Banking, Private Equity, Venture Capital, Wealth Management, or to launch finance-driven ventures.

GSBF Professional Training Programs & Career Tracks

CFFA — Finance Foundations

Build essential finance skills in accounting, Excel modeling, valuation basics, and investment thinking. Prepares beginners for all major finance tracks.

Investment Amounts: $150 – $300
Career Pathways / Tracks:
  • Investment Banking: Analyst internships, financial modeling support
  • Private Equity / VC: Junior investment analyst, portfolio research
  • Wealth Management: Client support, portfolio operations
Apply

CIP — Investment Professional

Develop advanced financial modeling, valuation, and deal analysis skills. Prepares candidates for professional roles across all finance tracks.

Investment Amounts: $400 – $800
Career Pathways / Tracks:
  • Investment Banking: Deal support, corporate finance analyst
  • Private Equity / VC: Associate, portfolio strategy roles
  • Wealth Management: Advisory roles, investment analysis
Apply

CAIP — Advanced Investment Practitioner

Elite training in deal structuring, portfolio strategy, and leadership. Prepares professionals for senior investment roles across multiple finance tracks.

Investment Amounts: $900 – $1,800
Career Pathways / Tracks:
  • Investment Banking: Senior analyst, associate, deal leadership
  • Private Equity / VC: Senior associate, investment manager
  • Wealth Management: Portfolio manager, senior advisory roles
  • GSBF Fellowship eligibility
