The MBA in Venture Strategy is the first program at Nolan University designed, delivered, and governed jointly by the Graduate School of Banking & Finance and the School of Entrepreneurship & Venture Creation.

🎯 Program vision

To develop the next generation of venture leaders who can seamlessly navigate both sides of the table—building transformative companies and deploying capital strategically.

This is not a traditional MBA. It is an intensive, immersive experience that builds dual competence in venture finance and venture creation, with a focus on African markets.
